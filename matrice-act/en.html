Matrice ACT

What matters to you, and the path toward it.

The matrix is a simple map of your experience. On the left: what moves you away from what matters. On the right: what moves you toward it. Up top: what happens inside (thoughts, emotions, memories). Down below: what can be seen (your gestures, your actions). Au centre : the one who notices all of it — you.

The matrix

Inside — mental experience
Outside — the five senses: what is seen, heard, done

How to fill your matrix

1. Start top left: what hooks you these days.

2. Then bottom left: what you do when it pulls hard.

3. Then top right: who and what matter to you. And the centre.

4. Finish with one very small step, bottom right — doable this week.

Psychological flexibility — six components

Psychological flexibility is three capacities that reinforce one another: being open to difficult experiences, present to the moment, and engaged toward what matters. Six components bring it to life — here is what each one lights up, with a question to reflect on. Answer at your own pace; it saves itself.

A tool from Authentique sexologie. It is meant to be used on your own and does not replace a consultation or follow-up with a sexologist. Your answers can be reviewed during a brief intervention: authentiquesexo.ca.

Based on the ACT Matrix (Polk and colleagues) and the psychological flexibility model (Hayes and colleagues).
